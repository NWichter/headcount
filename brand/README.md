# Headcount brand

- **Idea:** a tally of four strokes, closed by a lime fifth stroke, the GO sticker from the product. Four people is a maybe; the one that closes the count makes it happen. The strokes have Anton's stem width, so the mark sits with the wordmark (Anton, outlined to paths) like one line of type.
- **Colors:** Ink `#12110F` · Paper `#F2EDE1` · Lime `#B9F43C` (the closing stroke, always lime) · NO-GO `#FF4A1D` is for status stamps only, never in the logo.
- **Clear space:** keep free space of at least half the wordmark cap height (≈ 2.5 tally strokes) on every side; for the mark alone, the width of two strokes. Minimum size: logo 96 px wide, mark 24 px; below that use `favicon.svg` (micro icon: 3 bars on a 16 px grid, no shadow).
- **Do:** use `headcount-logo.svg` on Paper/light, `headcount-logo-dark.svg` on Ink/dark, and the lime stroke at its fixed angle with square-cut ends, ink keyline and hard shadow included.
- **Don't:** recolor the strokes or the lime, add or remove strokes, straighten or re-angle the strike, put the logo on lime or NO-GO red, or retype the wordmark in another font.
