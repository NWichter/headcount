08 / 13 · Why Solana

Why Solana

Strangers trust the rules,
not the organizer.

The program pays out only after the deadline, refunds everyone without the organizer, and allows cancelling only before the deadline.

01

A program vault only the rules can open

The organizer can't touch the money.

02

Sponsored by default

A transaction names its own fee payer, so attendees never hold SOL.

03

Scan-to-pay standard

Solana Pay QR works in the wallets people already have.

04

Check in and refund at once

Ed25519 verifies the door pass in the same transaction that returns the deposit.
